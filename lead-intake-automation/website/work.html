Fernhill Dental

The problem
Booking requests arrived by email and phone message, and urgent ones sat in the same pile as routine check-ups.
What we built
A clear booking-request form that asks about urgency, saves every request to one sheet, and emails the front desk straight away when someone reports pain.
The outcome
Requests live in one place, ordered by priority, and nothing relies on someone remembering to check an inbox.

Oakline Outfitters

The problem
A gear retailer wanted wholesale customers, but the sales team spent hours replying to very small orders.
What we built
A wholesale enquiry form that scores each buyer by order size and timeline, and alerts the sales lead only for the strongest matches.
The outcome
The team spends its time on serious buyers, while smaller enquiries get a helpful automatic reply.

Tidal Accounting

The problem
A small bookkeeping firm sounded like every other firm, and its prices were hard to find.
What we built
A new identity, a plain-language website, and three fixed-price packages with a simple enquiry form.
The outcome
Visitors understand what is offered and what it costs before they get in touch.
