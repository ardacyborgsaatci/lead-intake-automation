Privacy note.

Brightline Studio is a fictional demo company. This note describes how the demo contact form behaves.

What the form collects

Your name, email address, company, company size, budget, timeline and optional message. We also record the time you submitted it.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry and to rank it so the most relevant requests are answered first. The ranking is a simple points score based on the budget, company size and timeline you select.

Where it goes

Your details are sent to an automation workflow, saved as a row in a spreadsheet, and, for high-scoring enquiries, included in an email to the studio. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Deleting your details

To have your submission removed, send a message through the contact form asking for deletion and mention the email address you used.
