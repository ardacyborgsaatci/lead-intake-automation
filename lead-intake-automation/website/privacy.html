Privacy note

Brightline is a fictional company made for a portfolio project. This note describes what the demo contact form does with your details.

What the form collects

Your name, email address, company, company size, budget, timeline and an optional message. We also record the time you sent it.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry, and to score it so the likeliest projects are answered first. The score is a points total from the budget, company size and timeline you pick, plus whether your email is a business address.

Where it goes

The form sends your details to a Vercel function, which passes them to an n8n workflow. The workflow saves a row in a Google Sheet and, for high scores, emails the studio through Gmail. Your details are not sold or shared for marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad trackers. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page opens.

Deleting your details

To have your submission removed, send a message through the contact form asking for deletion and give the email address you used.
