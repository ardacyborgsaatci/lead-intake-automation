How lead intake works

Every enquiry takes the same four steps.

  1. 1. The visitor sends the form

    The page checks every field and drops spam.

  2. 2. Vercel passes it on

    The secret stays on the server, never in the browser.

  3. 3. n8n scores it

    Points for budget, size, timeline and email type.

  4. 4. You get the result

    A row in Google Sheets, plus an email if the lead is hot.

How fast does Brightline reply?

Within one business day. If it is a fit, a fixed-scope quote follows within three working days.

Which tools does lead intake use?

Your form sends data to n8n, which saves a row in Google Sheets and emails you through Gmail. The page runs on Vercel.

Who owns my lead data?

You do. Leads go to your own sheet and inbox. The workflow's run history, kept by n8n, is the only other copy.

Can I change the lead scoring rules?

Yes. We set the points and the hot threshold with you at the start, and change them whenever you ask.

What stops spam in lead intake?

A hidden field, a secret header between your site and n8n, and checks on every field. Junk never reaches your sheet.
