Services

Everything between a visitor and a customer.

Four services you can buy on their own or together. Budgets below are typical starting ranges, and every project gets a fixed-scope quote.

Websites

Typically $5k–$20k

Fast, accessible sites that are easy for your team to edit. We start with what a visitor needs to decide, then design every page around that.

  • Content structure and copy guidance
  • Responsive design for phones and desktops
  • Accessibility checks against WCAG 2.2
  • Performance budget and search-friendly markup

Lead capture & automation

Typically $1k–$5k

Forms that do more than send an email. Every enquiry is checked, scored against criteria you choose, saved, and routed to the right person.

  • Validation and spam protection
  • Scoring by budget, company size and timeline
  • Saved to Google Sheets or your CRM
  • Instant alerts for your best leads

The form on this site runs on the same system. Here is what happens to each submission:

  1. CheckRequired fields and spam are filtered
  2. ScorePoints for budget, size and timeline
  3. SaveA row is added to the lead sheet
  4. AlertHot leads trigger an email

  • Type choices and layout rules
