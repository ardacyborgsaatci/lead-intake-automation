About

We build the site and the system behind it.

Brightline Studio started from a simple frustration: too many good websites sent their enquiries into a black hole.

A visitor fills in a form, an email lands in a crowded inbox, and a week later someone replies. By then the customer has moved on. We set out to close that gap, so the website and the process behind it work as one.

We are a small, remote team of designers and developers. That means the people you speak to are the people who build your project.

How we work

Three principles.

Plain language

No jargon in proposals or updates. If we cannot explain it simply, we have not understood it.

Fixed scope

You get a clear scope and price before work starts, and changes are agreed in writing first.

You own it

Code, content, designs and accounts all belong to you. We hand over everything at launch.
